Click or tap a fret to sound it. Pluck always fades on its own; Hold rings only while you press and stops the moment you let go; Sustain keeps ringing after you release, so press the same fret again to stop it. Open strings show as rings, fretted notes as tiles, and the small dots mark the same reference frets you would find on a real neck. Dashed tiles marked ◆ are natural harmonics — lightly touching the open string there, rather than pressing it down, rings out a higher partial; the label shows what it actually sounds, which is usually not the same note you'd get by fretting that spot normally. The narrow "2½" column exists only for the 7th harmonic, whose true touch point falls between frets rather than on one. Highlighted notes get a ring; the root of a scale or chord gets a second color.
